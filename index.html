Read before using for prediction

Model assumptions and limits

  • All inputs are illustrativeThe 1–7 scores are reasoned estimates, not survey data. The shaded band on each bar shows the range if every score were half a point higher or lower.
  • Weights are not estimated from datav2 effect sizes follow published research directions (job characteristics link strongly to motivation, weakly to objective performance; satisfaction links modestly to turnover), but the exact numbers are set by reasoning. Calibrate with real survey scores and outcomes before using it to predict.
  • MPS multiplies scoresHackman & Oldham's formula exaggerates gaps (16 vs 192). Research finds a simple additive index predicts about as well. It is kept because it is the classic model.
  • Autonomy appears in both theoriesJob autonomy (what the role allows) and felt autonomy (what people experience) are separate inputs and have different values in v2.
  • What the model leaves outHeadcount decisions, training cost, governance of AI rule changes, job insecurity, pay and the job market. Each can change real outcomes.
