Read before using for prediction

Model assumptions and limits

  • Autonomy appears in both JCM and SDTIn v1 the two autonomy inputs share the same defaults, so autonomy counts twice. They are separate sliders: job autonomy is what the role allows, felt autonomy is the person's experience. With real data, measure each with its own scale.
  • Only Volume and Retention change over timeQuality and Motivation are the same at 3 and 12 months by design. The "Automate only wins early" result comes from volume alone (a 3.3-point gap in v1).
  • Higher autonomy lowers volume, by assumption0.75 × (7 − Autonomy) says people with more discretion spend time on judgment. This is a modelling choice; change the coefficient to test it.
  • The exit door is illustrative+N is Retention risk ÷ 8.5. It signals turnover pressure, not a resignation forecast.
  • Not yet calibratedWeights are set by reasoning. To use this for prediction, collect JCM/SDT survey scores and real outcomes (throughput, error rate, exits), estimate the weights by regression and enter them above.
